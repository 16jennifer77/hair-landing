When to come in

Catch thinning while the follicle is still alive

  • Thinning at the crown, temples, or part line
  • More hair in the drain, the brush, or on the pillow
  • Shedding after stress, illness, or hormonal change
  • Early graying or patchy pigment loss
  • Plateaued on minoxidil or finasteride alone

A dormant follicle can often be woken. A dead one cannot. The earlier the exam, the more options stay on the table.

We treat the follicle like any other organ: examine it first, measure it honestly, and only then decide what it needs.

Our approach

What we will and will not tell you

Hair restoration is full of overpromises. Our plans are built to survive an honest follow-up photo.

  • A

    An exam before any treatment

    Nobody is prescribed microneedling, PRP, or medication before a nurse has examined the scalp and a physician has reviewed the plan. Not every treatment suits every head.

  • B

    Results vary, and we say so

    Response depends on the cause of loss, how early we catch it, and how consistently the plan is followed. We will show you your own photos, not someone else's.

  • C

    Medication only where it belongs

    Finasteride and oral minoxidil are prescription drugs with real considerations. A licensed clinician decides if they are appropriate for you, and you hear the trade-offs first.

  • D

    No pressure at the consult

    The free nurse consult ends with a written plan and prices. Take it home. The follicles will still be there tomorrow, and so will we.
